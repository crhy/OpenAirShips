# Old files

Earlier OpenAirShips models, kept for history and reference. They are **not** the current design. For that, see [`CurrentModel/`](../CurrentModel) and its [changelog](../CurrentModel/CHANGELOG.md).

- `CAD/`: FreeCAD models of earlier pie slices.
  - `airship pie slice 125.FCStd` is the direct ancestor of the 926 bench model.
  - The 2-byte 1024 files are empty placeholders; the real model is behind the Google link.
- `PrintFiles/`: earlier printable STLs.
  - `PieSlice1224.stl`
  - the 926 development revisions A–D. **RevB and revC have a duct leak; don't print them.**
- The top-level files are older pie-slice, component and obelisk experiments, logos and prototype photos.
- `PrintFiles/926/` and `CAD/926/`: the released 926 model (v0.1.3 bench and Kobra Max slices, v0.2 double-Kobra pieces), replaced in October 2026 by the 1026 model (flush fan housing, lighter keel).
