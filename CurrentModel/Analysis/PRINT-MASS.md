# Printed mass budget (1026 v0.2.1): every committed print STL, measured

Generated by `print_mass.py` from the STL meshes that ship — not from the CAD kernel, and not from a slicer. Rerun it after changing the model.

This is the recorded baseline the triage on [crhy/OpenAirShips#1](https://github.com/crhy/OpenAirShips/issues/1) asks for: the current mass, the material, and the wall and nozzle settings behind it. The CAD revision, the weighing and the load tests are not here.

**How it is measured**

- 59 binary STL files read directly: 80-byte header, uint32 triangle count at bytes 80:84, 50-byte records with the vertices at bytes 12:48.
- Every undirected edge is used exactly twice in all of them, so all are watertight, as `CHANGELOG.md:34` and `SimplifiedSlice/README.md:94` claim. A mesh with gaps is refused, not reported.
- Volume and area are integrated per triangle in float64; mass = volume × density.
- `--check` recomputes this document from the STLs and fails if it differs from the committed one, so the numbers cannot drift away from the meshes.
- **Mean wall** is 2 × volume ÷ area: the average thickness the mesh's own skin carries. The two intake tubes average 0.858 and 0.861 mm — they are the only printed parts with no lattice in them, so their mean is the wall itself. Hull slices average 0.51–0.82 mm, below the wall, because the lattice windows add surface without adding material.

## What halving the wall would do

- The issue asks for the model at half the current thickness. The current wall is **0.86 mm** (2 perimeters of 0.45 mm at 0.2 mm layers), and `DESIGN-CONSTRAINTS.md:20` records that as **the minimum that prints airtight**, with nothing structural thinner.
- Half of it, 0.43 mm, is one 0.45 mm perimeter: below that minimum. The shaft, the fan housing and the ducts would stop being airtight, and the fan's pressure depends on them being airtight (`DESIGN-CONSTRAINTS.md:12`, `SimplifiedSlice/README.md:118`).
- The meshes are already there: the two intake tubes, which are plain Ø66 tubes of that wall and nothing else, average 0.858 and 0.861 mm — the 0.86 mm the constraints set, to three decimal places. There is no thicker wall to halve.
- So the grams cannot come from wall thickness without breaking a rule the design owner set (`DESIGN-CONSTRAINTS.md:3`). They come from **material**, **part count** and **geometry**:
  - **Material:** the same geometry at 0.60 instead of 1.24 g/cm³ is 2.07 times lighter. The double Kobra's 474.0 cm³ of hull is 587.8 g in PLA and 284.4 g in full foam.
  - **Part count:** 4 thrusters instead of 8 takes the rings, mounts, stems and gears from 225.3 cm³ to 137.5 cm³, and the 4-thruster hull is 30.4 cm³ lighter than the 8-thruster one.
  - **Geometry:** webs at 1.2 mm instead of 2.0 mm on the v0.2 build (`DESIGN-CONSTRAINTS.md:26`), the number of slices and pieces, and the intake tubes: 127.1 cm³ of tube is more than any one slice set.

### The issue's 24 g anchor

One 8-thruster bench slice — the 1/8 component — measures 26.79 cm³: 33.2 g in PLA, 21.4 g in moderate foam, 16.1 g in full foam. The comment's "down to 24 grams" sits between moderate and full foam, so a gram figure only means something beside its material and its settings, which is what this document records for every part.

## Not measured here
