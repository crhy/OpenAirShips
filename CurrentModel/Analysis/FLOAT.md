# Will it float? Mass, hydrogen lift and fan thrust

Generated by `float_budget.py` from the current CAD (every volume comes from a tessellated STEP). Rerun it after changing the model.

**Assumptions**
- **Hydrogen:** net lift 1.12 g per litre (20 °C, sea level). The gas cells fill all the free space inside the skin, outside the shaft, fan housing and ducts.
- **Gas cells:** 12 µm LDPE film (11 g/m²), 1.3 × the hull's surface area.
- **Materials:**
  - Hull, thruster rings and servo mounts are printed in the material shown.
  - Stems, gears and the fan hatch stay standard PLA (1.24 g/cm³); the impeller is PETG.
  - Lightweight (foaming) PLA: 0.80 g/cm³ with moderate foam, 0.60 with full foam.
- **Airborne mass:** tethered, so the battery, ESC and controller stay on the bench.
- **Thrust:** the fan's full thrust with every jet pointing down, from the airflow model (2207 1750 KV on 4S, shrouded fan). The same fan at every size.
- **x1.9 (Kobra Max):** only the hull grows. Walls, ribs, fan, ducts, thrusters and servos keep their size.

- **Lift ÷ mass:** how close the hydrogen alone is to floating the model. 100% means it floats with no fan.
- **Lift + thrust − mass:** positive means the fan plus the hydrogen can hold the model up, while the fan runs.

## How big would it have to be to float on hydrogen alone?

Lift grows with size³. The thin-walled hull grows much more slowly, because its walls and ribs keep their thickness (measured below between x1 and x1.9). The fan, servos and wiring stay the same.

| Design | Hull material | Floats at scale | Hull diameter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 thrusters | LW-PLA, moderate foam | x4.2 (hull mass grows as S^0.95) | 1.8 m |
| 8 thrusters | LW-PLA, full foam | x3.9 (hull mass grows as S^0.95) | 1.6 m |
| 4 thrusters | LW-PLA, moderate foam | x3.8 (hull mass grows as S^0.92) | 1.6 m |
| 4 thrusters | LW-PLA, full foam | x3.5 (hull mass grows as S^0.92) | 1.5 m |

These keep today's 0.86 mm walls and 2 mm ribs. A real hull that size would need thicker ribs, or a frame and fabric envelope instead of a printed skin, so treat them as lower bounds.
