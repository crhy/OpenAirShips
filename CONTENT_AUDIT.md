## Whitepapers and repository artifacts

- The 2019 whitepaper is copied without modification to `web/assets/docs/openairships-whitepaper-2019.pdf`.
- The 2026 51-page builder guide is copied without modification to `web/assets/docs/openairships-builder-guide.pdf`.
- The current printable wedge is copied to `web/assets/models/openairship-wedge.stl`.
- The source FreeCAD file remains in `CurrentModel/CAD Files/airship pie slice 125.FCStd`.
- Original art, videos, project models and repository history remain in their existing top-level folders. The redesign copies selected web-optimized assets; it does not delete the archive.

## Editorial policy used in the remodel

No historical statement was removed simply because it was abrasive, disputed or unverified. Where a statement could be mistaken for established fact, the new copy identifies it as a project claim, historic position or design target and states what evidence is missing. This keeps the information while making the public site more credible.

## Link health at migration

Links were checked on 2026-09-12. The current collaboration, donation, download, social, and crowdfunding destinations were reachable. Two historical publisher URLs—the Slashdot submission and the 2013 Popular Science item—returned 404 and 410 respectively. Both are retained as historical data and explicitly labeled in the page copy. The Internet Archive was temporarily unavailable during the audit, so archived replacements could not be confirmed. Bookshop, Indiegogo, and Quora rejected automated checks with HTTP 403; that result does not establish that their browser-facing pages are unavailable.
