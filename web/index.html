The Goal

Our goal at OpenAirShips.com is to provide extremely low-cost housing and transportation for every individual and family.

​This will have a profound and positive impact on our lives and health by drastically lowering prices for all goods, services, and real estate globally.

The Full Plan

Our Goal

OpenAirShips' goal is to completely replace our current civilization and way of life with a superior Open Source utopia.

The Plan

Our plan is to develop small, modular, open source, 3d printable autonomous AirShips and then scale up using off-the-shelf components.

The long term goal is to provide nearly free travel, housing, electricity, internet, freedom, and clean water to every human being.
