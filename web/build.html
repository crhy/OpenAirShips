Build the 1026 model v0.2.1

The first buildable OpenAirShip. New in v0.2: a 1.58 m self-contained ship, printed in pieces on a Kobra Max, that floats on hydrogen alone. The 415 mm and 790 mm tethered models are here too. Every file is free and open source.

How it works

Printed slices plug together into a smooth ellipsoid hull: eight 45° slices for the tethered models, twelve 30° slices for v0.2. Inside, everything except the air path is a lattice of large oval cells with hollow junctions, and every web is 2 mm wide (1.2 mm on v0.2).

A fan at the bottom of a smooth Ø66 central intake pulls air in over the rounded top. The hull's own housing wall forms the fan's shroud. The fan pushes the air into ducts that run inside the skin to the equator.

At each thruster seam a swivelling Dyson-style air-multiplier ring turns the duct's air into a vectored jet, turned ±90° by a servo. The thrusters sit exactly on the equator, so they give lift, yaw, surge and sway without pitching the ship.

The 4-thruster design uses bigger ducts and rings (twice the flow each) and alternating left and right slices. It makes slightly more thrust than 8 thrusters, and it's lighter.

Will it float?

Yes: the double-Kobra build floats. In full-foam lightweight PLA with a 1500 mAh LiPo, and with its ESP32 and all its electronics on board, it weighs 977 g. Its 1022 L of hydrogen lifts 1145 g, so it has 168 g to spare. The fan is only needed to manoeuvre, and it flies about 44 minutes, or 83 on an 18650 pack (153 g to spare). Print it in full foam: in moderate foam it only just floats. On helium it still floats, with about 84 g to spare.

Corrected in v0.2.1: the v0.2 budget undercounted the double-Kobra hull (350 cm³ instead of 502), so it was about 90 g heavier than we said. The new flush housing and lattice save 28 cm³, and these are the corrected numbers.

The smaller tethered models don't float on gas alone, but they can hover on their fan. The 4-thruster Kobra Max model in full-foam lightweight PLA (503 g, 124 L of hydrogen) has 55 g to spare, and the 4-thruster bench model (368 g) has 73 g.

The full mass, hydrogen and thrust budget covers every version, material and battery.
